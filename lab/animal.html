Animals Gallery

Explore various animals and their characteristics.

Lion

The lion is a large cat native to Africa and India, known for its mane and social pride structure.

Tiger

The tiger is the largest living cat species, recognizable by its striped fur.

Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals, known for their intelligence and trunks.

Giraffe

The giraffe is the tallest terrestrial animal, with a long neck and spotted coat.

Zebra

Zebras are African equines with unique black-and-white stripes.

Monkey

Monkeys are intelligent primates belonging to the infraorder Simiiformes.
