Animals Gallery

Explore various animals and their characteristics.

Lion

The lion is known as the king of the jungle. It is a large carnivorous feline with a majestic mane.

Tiger

The tiger is the largest species among the Felidae, recognizable for dark vertical stripes.

Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth, known for their intelligence and trunks.

Giraffe

The giraffe is the tallest living terrestrial animal, famous for its long neck and legs.

Zebra

Zebras are known for their distinctive black and white striped coats used for camouflage.

Monkey

Monkeys are intelligent primates known for their agility and playful behavior.
